📌 Functions vs. One-to-One

Function Rule: Every x has exactly one y. x-values cannot map to different outputs.
✅ Pass: (4, 8) & (3, 7)
✅ Pass: (4, 8) & (4, 8) — Same point listed twice is fine.
❌ Fail: (4, 8) & (4, 9) — One x points to two different y's.

One-to-One (1-1) Rule: No two distinct inputs share the same output. y-values cannot repeat.
✅ Pass: (4, 8) & (3, 7)
✅ Pass: (4, 8) & (4, 8) — Same point listed twice is fine.
❌ Fail: (4, 8) & (2, 8) — Different x's sharing the same y.

📈 Vertical & Horizontal Line Tests

Passes VLT, Fails HLT:
y is a function, but not one-to-one.

Passes HLT, Fails VLT:
y is not a function of x.

Passes both VLT & HLT:
y is a one-to-one function.

⚖️ Perpendicular Lines

Perpendicular lines have opposite reciprocal slopes.

If Line L has a slope of 2/3, the perpendicular slope is -3/2.
